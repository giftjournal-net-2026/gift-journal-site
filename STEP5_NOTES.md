## Product policy
`data/products.ts` remains empty. Do not add fabricated prices, ratings, review counts, availability or affiliate URLs. Populate products only after the retailer/program and destination are verified.

## Affiliate network direction
The architecture is retailer/network agnostic. Initial research should prioritize Amazon Associates plus publisher networks such as impact.com and Awin, then add direct merchant programs where the audience/product fit is strong.
